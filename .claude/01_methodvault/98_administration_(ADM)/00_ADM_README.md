
ADM files in this vault hold non-engineering material about the method itself —
open items, blockers, milestones. They are not decisions and not evidence.

The folder is empty today. The open items of this method are not free-floating:
each one lives in the `### Follow-ups` or `### Accepted residuals` subsection of
the decision that produced it, and lifting them into a register here would mean
maintaining the same item twice. An ADM note earns its existence when something
has to be tracked that no single decision owns.

The folder also carries the vault: `validate_vault.py` recognises a vault root
by three domain folders, and this is one of them.
